Who makes this

Built by two people, not a plugin factory.

We're Clay & Kelsy — a queer, woman-owned, two-artist duo. We don't just build these synths; we make the music with them. Every wave shape was sampled in our own studio, every preset played until it felt right. That's the part Arturia and Native Instruments can't copy: these instruments have taste because they were made by people with something to say.

The music we make has been featured in Music Connection, EARMILK, Naluda, V13 & Apricot — Music Connection even called the Osc Collection “worthy of your attention.”

Not a re-skin.
A ground-up rebuild.

The same sound you know, rebuilt as a native instrument you own — with modern, expressive playing the Kontakt version simply couldn't do.

Fully standalone
Native VST3 & AU — plus a standalone app that plays with no DAW open at all.
Yours outright
A one-time plug-in, signed & notarized. Mac (Apple Silicon + Intel) & Windows. No Kontakt, no Native Access.
Full MPE
Per-note pitch, pressure & slide — Seaboard, LinnStrument, Osmose and Push all play it properly.
Per-note expression
Polyphonic aftertouch and a per-note mod matrix — route pressure, slide & velocity to almost anything.
MIDI-Learn anything
Right-click any control to bind it to a CC. The mapping saves with the session and travels inside shared presets.
A living interface
The artwork reacts to how hard you play — colors bloom and cycle as you hold notes. MPE-aware. Dark mode too.
Share presets as files
Make a patch, send the file to a bandmate, drop it in a folder. Sessions recall the patch, your MIDI maps, everything.
True legato & glide
Mono & legato modes with real legato, plus key glide / portamento in three modes.
Loads instantly
A tiny native footprint — none of the wait of pulling a big sample library into Kontakt.
